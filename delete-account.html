Delete your FitVector account and data

FitVector: Workout & Macros · by BamaApps LLC

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open FitVector and go to the You tab.
  2. Tap the settings (gear) button to open Profile.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and all of its server data are deleted immediately.

Can't open the app?

Email hello@bamaappsllc.com with the subject "Delete my FitVector account" and your friend code (shown in the app) or first name. We'll delete the account within 7 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your anonymous account identifier and the first name you shared (if any)
  • Your friend code and friends list
  • Your leaderboard totals (workouts, streak, volume)
  • Your AI coach usage counts

Nothing is kept after deletion. We never store your workouts, food log, photos or health data on our server, and AI coach conversations are not stored.

Delete the data on your phone (without deleting your account)

  • In the app: Profile → Erase all data removes your profile, workouts, food log, measurements and photos from the phone. You can also simply uninstall the app.
  • Health Connect: open the Health Connect app → App permissions → FitVector → Delete app data.
  • Backups: remove them in Google Drive → Storage → Backups (Android) or iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage (iPhone).
