Privacy Policy

Last updated October 15, 2026 · Applies to FitVector for iPhone and Android

FitVector is built so your fitness data stays yours. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't use tracking or analytics SDKs.

Data that stays on your phone

Your profile (age, height, weight, goals, diet), workouts, lifts, food log, water, body measurements, military PT test scores, reflections and progress photos are stored on your device. We can't see them.

Apple Health (iPhone)

If you connect Apple Health, FitVector reads data such as weight, height, age, sex, steps, active energy, resting heart rate and sleep, and can write your weigh-ins, workouts, nutrition and water. Health data is used only to provide features inside the app. It is never sent to our server, never shared with third parties, and never used for advertising or marketing. You can change access anytime in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

Health Connect (Android)

If you connect Health Connect, FitVector can read weight, body fat, steps, active calories burned, sleep and resting heart rate, and can write your weigh-ins, body fat, workouts (exercise sessions and active calories), nutrition and hydration. We use this data only to show your activity, recovery and progress inside the app and to keep your logs in sync with your other health apps.

  • Health Connect data is processed on your phone. It is never sent to our server, never sold, never shared with third parties, and never used for advertising, marketing, credit or insurance decisions.
  • FitVector's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • You can review or revoke access anytime in the Health Connect app (or Android Settings → Security & privacy → Privacy → Health Connect).

Backups

iPhone: if you use iCloud backup, a copy of your data and photos is saved to your own private iCloud Drive, governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Android: your data and photos are included in Android's encrypted backup to your own Google account (if backup is on in your phone's settings), governed by Google's privacy policy. You can also save a backup file yourself and choose where to keep it. We have no access to either.

Optional account

An account is optional and enables friend leaderboards and the cloud AI coach. On iPhone you sign in with Apple; on Android the app creates an anonymous account for your install. If you sign in, our server stores:

  • An anonymous identifier (from Apple, or a random ID created by the app on Android), and the first name you choose to share (if any)
  • Your friend code and the friends you add
  • Monthly totals shown on leaderboards: workouts completed, streak and total volume lifted
  • A daily count of AI coach messages (to enforce fair-use limits)

We don't receive your email address, and we don't store your workouts, food log or health data on the server.

AI coach

On iPhones that support Apple Intelligence, the coach can run entirely on your device. Otherwise, if you're signed in, your coach messages and a short summary of your goals and recent training are sent to our server and processed by Anthropic's Claude API to generate a reply. Health Connect data is not included. We don't store the conversation. Anthropic processes this data as our service provider and does not use it to train its models.

Food and exercise lookups

When you search foods or scan a barcode, the search term or barcode is sent to the USDA FoodData Central or Open Food Facts databases (sometimes through our server). Exercise and research information comes from wger and PubMed. No personal information is included in these requests.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only to scan food barcodes and take progress photos you choose to save. On Android, barcode scanning uses Google's code scanner in Google Play services, which returns only the barcode number to FitVector. Photos stay on your device (and in your own backup, if enabled).

Subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Apple (iPhone) or Google Play (Android). We never see your payment details.

Deleting your data

  • Delete your account and all server data anytime: Profile → Account → Delete account.
  • Erase everything on your phone: Profile → Erase all data, or delete the app.
  • Remove your iCloud backup in iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage, or your Android backup in Google Drive → Storage → Backups.
  • Remove FitVector's Health Connect data in the Health Connect app → App permissions → FitVector → Delete app data.

Children

FitVector is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
